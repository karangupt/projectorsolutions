FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can you deliver a projector?

In most cases we can deliver and set up a projector on the same day within Navi Mumbai and nearby areas, subject to availability. For events, we recommend booking at least 24–48 hours in advance.

Do you provide technical staff for setup?

Yes, our team handles delivery, installation, and testing on-site, and technical support staff can stay for the duration of larger events on request.

What is included in the rental price?

The rental typically includes the projector, cabling, and setup support. Screens and sound systems are available as add-ons and can be bundled into one quote.

Can I rent a projector for just one day?

Yes, we offer flexible rental durations from a single day to multi-day and weekly bookings for longer conferences or productions.
